Case study

MoodifySDK

Moodify's apps relied on another company's ad SDK. When that ended in December 2025, the after call screen and its revenue went with it. I proposed we build our own, then built it.

My part
Proposal, architecture, build, review, QA, integration
Built with
Kotlin, WorkManager, AdMob, Play Billing, Claude Code
Built for
VoiceBox, PaperClip, Shuffl
Outcome
Paid licence and integration; the client owns it now

Calls I made

  1. Dropped the always on service for a receiver and a background worker.The old service died with one swipe and a tap. The new setup is woken by Android itself, so closing the app doesn't kill it.

  2. Built and tested everything in a separate playground app first.No live app got touched until the SDK worked on its own.

  3. Scoped the Google Play compliance upgrades as separate paid work.Kept the original contract clean and the client clear on cost.
